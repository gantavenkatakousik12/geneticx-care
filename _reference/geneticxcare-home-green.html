Genetics is part of healthcare

Understanding genetic factors helps families decide what comes next

Many health conditions may involve genetic or familial factors. Understanding those factors can help individuals and families make better-informed healthcare decisions.

Patient-centeredWe consider the individual, their family and their healthcare needs, not just a test result.

Evidence-informedOur approach is based on established principles of clinical genetics and available scientific evidence.

CollaborativeGenetics is most useful when it works alongside appropriate medical and specialist care.

ResponsibleWe do not promote unnecessary genetic testing or promise outcomes that genetic testing cannot provide.

About GeneticxCare
How GeneticxCare Works

A clear pathway from your first concern to the next step

  1. 01

    Understand

    We begin with your concern, medical history and relevant family history.

  2. 02

    Assess

    We review the information to understand whether genetics may be relevant.

  3. 03

    Explain

    We discuss the genetic considerations, testing options and what they may mean for you.

  4. 04

    Guide

    When needed, we guide you toward the appropriate diagnostic service, specialist or healthcare pathway.
